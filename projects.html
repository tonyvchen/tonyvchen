Vehicles / 2024–2025

Competition electric vehicle

  • Science Olympiad event: build a vehicle propelled solely by electrical energy that runs down a track as fast as possible and stops as close as it can to a target point.
  • Scoring adds a time penalty to a distance penalty and the lowest score wins, so speed and stopping accuracy trade directly against each other.
  • The target distance is anywhere from 7 to 10 m and isn't announced until after impound, so the vehicle has to be recalibrated to an unknown distance on the spot, then run twice inside an 8-minute window.
  • Built around an Arduino-controlled drivetrain on a 3D-printed chassis, with braking triggered in software so a new target distance could be entered between runs.
Target distance 7–10 m, announced at event Event time 8 min, 2 runs

Vehicles / 2024–2025

Custom RC race car

  • 3D-printed chassis with a dual-motor drivetrain and independent electronic speed controllers.
  • Ackermann steering geometry so the inner and outer wheels track at different optimal angles, which tightens the turning radius and keeps the tires from scrubbing in corners.
  • 15° caster angle for self-aligning torque and straight-line stability at speed.
  • Printed structural parts combined with sourced rod ends for the load-bearing linkages.
Power 5200 mAh / 11.1 V Drive Dual motor, 2× ESC
